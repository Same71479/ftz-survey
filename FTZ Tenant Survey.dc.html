Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) Tenant Survey

WareSpace is exploring whether Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) capabilities at select locations could provide meaningful financial and operational benefits to our tenants.

For businesses that import products, components, materials or inventory from outside the United States, operating within an activated FTZ may allow certain customs duties to be deferred while goods remain in the zone and, depending on how the goods are ultimately used or sold, potentially reduced or eliminated.

We would love to better understand whether FTZ capabilities could benefit your business. This survey should take less than 3 minutes.
